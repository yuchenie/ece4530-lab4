3. VERIFICATION METHODS
=======================

DC and AC
---------
The DC servo remains connected, and the original 9 kOhm feedback
branch is isolated. The AC input magnitude is 1 V and CL is 1 pF.
The ADE run used for the reported DC/AC results is Interactive.8
(older compensation). The run with the final C_MILLER / R_ZERO is
[FILL IN run name].

Output resistance
-----------------
Set the input AC magnitude to zero. Inject 1 A AC from ground into
the output, with zero DC current. The output-voltage magnitude then
gives the output impedance.

Measured values:
    1 Hz:    1.81116 kOhm
    100 Hz:  1.79813 kOhm

CMRR
----
Open loop, DC servo connected. A series source at the inverting input
has its AC magnitude set to acm_cm. AC analysis with acm_in = 1,
acm_cm = 0 gives Avd; with acm_in = 0, acm_cm = 1 gives Acm.
    CMRR = Avd(dB) - Acm(dB) = 100.35 dB at low frequency

PSRR
----
Same testbench with acm_vdd = 1 (V3 AC) and acm_in = acm_cm = 0 gives
Add. Add is about +0.2 dB (0.2066 dB), so PSRR is approximately Avd(f).
    PSRR = Avd(dB) - Add(dB) = 91.8 dB at low frequency
(Avd was 92 dB in this measurement.)

Output current range
--------------------
I19 (idc) must be a shunt source from OUT to GND, not in series with the
out-to-E1 sense wire. DC sweep iout from negative to positive; plot
Vout and find where it moves 100 mV from the no-load value.
    Max (source):  +65.88 mA
    Min (sink):    -1.487 mA

Open-loop output swing
----------------------
Keep the DC servo and apply a 100 Hz sine wave. Sweep input peak
amplitude through 1, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, and 40 uV. Run each simulation
for 30 ms and analyze the final cycle, from 20 to 30 ms.

Gain is calculated from the fundamental amplitude using 1,000 samples
over that cycle. Output swing is the measured peak-to-peak voltage.

Input peak amplitude     Output swing      Fundamental gain
------------------------------------------------------------
10 uV                    0.81055 Vpp        92.14265 dB
15 uV                    1.21023 Vpp        92.14462 dB
20 uV                    1.50797 Vpp        91.81311 dB
30 uV                    1.82894 Vpp        90.45289 dB
40 uV                    2.04179 Vpp        89.11842 dB
------------------------------------------------------------

At 40 uV input, compression is approximately 3.024 dB relative to the
10 uV reference. The 3 dB boundary lies between the tested 30 and
40 uV amplitudes. Report the conservative verified swing of at least
1.51 Vpp unless the boundary is refined.

The compression column in validation_metrics.txt uses the 1 uV
reference instead. Its gain is about 0.0604 dB below the 10 uV
reference. State the reference amplitude when quoting compression.


4. CLOSED-LOOP VERIFICATION
==========================

Gain of 10
----------
RF = 9 kOhm; RG = 1 kOhm connected to a 1.4 V reference.
Input step: 1.35 V to 1.45 V. Load: 1 pF.

Output levels             0.89980 V to 1.89897 V
10-90% rise time           97.11 ns
90-10% fall time           103.38 ns
Average rising slope      8.23 V/us
Average falling slope    -7.73 V/us

These slopes are averages over the 10-90% transition. They are not
slew-rate measurements taken from the steepest linear segment.
The slew rate (steepest slope of the output edge, via the derivative
of Vout) is 11.85 V/us, higher than the averages above.

Lab divider testbench (alternate gain-of-10)
--------------------------------------------
R1 = 1.715 kOhm to VDD, R2 = 2.401 kOhm to GND, R3 = 9 kOhm feedback.
The Thevenin reference is V_th = 1.458 V, so
    Vout = 10 * Vin - 9 * V_th = 10 * Vin - 13.125 (V)
A 1.35 V to 1.45 V input step gives about 0.38 V to 1.38 V here, versus
0.90 V to 1.90 V with RG tied to the 1.4 V reference above.

Closed-loop input range
-----------------------
Method: DC sweep of the input (1.2 V to 1.6 V, 2 mV steps) on the
gain-of-10 circuit. Find where the slope of the transfer curve (deriv)
drops 1 dB, i.e. 10 * 10^(-1/20) = 8.9 V/V.
    Range: 1.32 V to 1.56 V (about -80 mV / +160 mV about 1.4 V)
    Symmetric about 1.4 V: about +/-80 mV
A 100 Hz transient confirmation was not recorded.

rate and closed-loop bandwidth are assumed unchanged by the
compensation retune).
