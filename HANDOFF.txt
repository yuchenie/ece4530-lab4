3. VERIFICATION METHODS
=======================

DC and AC
---------
The DC servo remains connected, and the original 9 kOhm feedback
branch is isolated. The AC input magnitude is 1 V and CL is 1 pF.
The final ADE run used for the reported DC/AC results is Interactive.8.

Output resistance
-----------------
Set the input AC magnitude to zero. Inject 1 A AC from ground into
the output, with zero DC current. The output-voltage magnitude then
gives the output impedance.

Measured values:
    1 Hz:    1.81116 kOhm
    100 Hz:  1.79813 kOhm

Open-loop output swing
----------------------
Keep the DC servo and apply a 100 Hz sine wave. Sweep input peak
amplitude through 1, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, and 40 uV. Run each simulation
for 30 ms and analyze the final cycle, from 20 to 30 ms.

Gain is calculated from the fundamental amplitude using 1,000 samples
over that cycle. Output swing is the measured peak-to-peak voltage.

Input peak amplitude     Output swing      Fundamental gain
------------------------------------------------------------
10 uV                    0.81055 Vpp        92.14265 dB
15 uV                    1.21023 Vpp        92.14462 dB
20 uV                    1.50797 Vpp        91.81311 dB
30 uV                    1.82894 Vpp        90.45289 dB
40 uV                    2.04179 Vpp        89.11842 dB
------------------------------------------------------------

At 40 uV input, compression is approximately 3.024 dB relative to the
10 uV reference. The 3 dB boundary lies between the tested 30 and
40 uV amplitudes. Report the conservative verified swing of at least
1.51 Vpp unless the boundary is refined.

The compression column in validation_metrics.txt uses the 1 uV
reference instead. Its gain is about 0.0604 dB below the 10 uV
reference. State the reference amplitude when quoting compression.

5. NOTES FOR THE NEXT TEAM MEMBER
================================

- The main requirements pass, but the remaining margin is modest:
  about 3.44 degrees of phase margin and 5.67 MHz of bandwidth.
  Further optimization can trade device area and power for margin.

- The full lab report still needs CMRR, PSRR, output-current range,
  maximum stable load, closed-loop input range, and the layout work
  required for ECE 5530.

- Results from earlier versions, including the 2 pF and 5 pF load
  tests, do not characterize this final circuit.

- Keep the Cadence library name "lab4" when using the project.
  Update the local cds.lib path to match your own checkout.

- The reference netlist and saved measurements describe the validated
  circuit above. After changing the schematic, regenerate the netlist
  and rerun the relevant checks before reusing these numbers.
